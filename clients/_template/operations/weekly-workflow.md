# Weekly Workflow Template

## Monday

- Publish a replay, clip, or insight from the previous episode.
- Pull forward one durable lesson that will still matter next month.

## Tuesday

- Publish a thought-leadership post from the primary voice of the brand.
- Tie the post to one content pillar and one commercial bridge.

## Wednesday

- Publish or refresh event promotion for the upcoming live show.
- Lead with the durable strategic theme, not the headline of the week.

## Thursday

- Publish a supporting framework, educational asset, or strategic company-page post.

## Friday

- Run live promotion.
- Publish replay follow-up after the show.

## Weekend / Optional

- Use for extra clips, commentary, breaking-news reactions, or audience engagement.
