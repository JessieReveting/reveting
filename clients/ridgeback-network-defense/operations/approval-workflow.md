# Ridgeback Approval Workflow

## Required Approvals

1. Weekly strategic theme
2. Event description and live promotion copy
3. Post-live recap and repurposed assets

## Approval Rules

- Do not generate final event copy until the strategic theme is approved.
- Do not make the public-facing topics a direct copy of article headlines or CVE names.
- Keep the Breaking Threat Brief editable until shortly before the show.
- Treat the repo as the durable planning source and the workbook as the execution surface.
