# Ridgeback Weekly Workflow

## Monday

- Publish a replay insight or short clip from the previous Breach of Protocol episode.
- Choose a takeaway that reinforces Ridgeback's strategic point of view.

## Tuesday

- Publish a Scott Fogarty thought-leadership post.
- Focus on cyber strategy, leadership, preemptive defense, or the changing tempo of conflict.

## Wednesday

- Publish the upcoming Breach of Protocol event post.
- Lead with the durable strategic issue for Friday's show.
- Reserve the Breaking Threat Brief details for a late-week refresh if needed.

## Thursday

- Publish an educational or strategic company-page post.
- Use a framework, board-level perspective, or operator takeaway that supports Friday's live conversation.

## Friday

- Publish a morning reminder for the 3:00 PM ET show.
- Publish a final live reminder close to airtime.
- Publish the replay link and a high-value takeaway after the show when available.

## Weekend / Optional

- Use for replay distribution, polling, breaking cyber news, an additional clip, or Scott commentary.
