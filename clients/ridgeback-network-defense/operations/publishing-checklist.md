# Ridgeback Publishing Checklist

- Confirm the event title uses a durable strategic theme.
- Confirm the description includes discussion angles plus a clearly labeled Breaking Threat Brief section.
- Confirm the post advances one approved Ridgeback content pillar.
- Confirm the CTA fits the draft and does not overpower the analysis.
- Confirm the workbook row has the right owner, asset need, and status values.
- Confirm operator-entered links are preserved before any repo-to-sheet sync runs.
