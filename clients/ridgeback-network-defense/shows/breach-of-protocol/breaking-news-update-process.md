# Breaking News Update Process

## Purpose

Keep the live event publishable early in the week without letting the public framing become stale.

## Process

1. Approve the durable strategic theme first.
2. Draft the event around the strategic issue and planned discussion angles.
3. Leave the Breaking Threat Brief section intentionally updateable.
4. Late Thursday or Friday morning, swap in the most important cyberattack, vulnerability, AI development, or policy story of the week.
5. Do not rewrite the entire event title or structure unless the strategic theme itself changes.
