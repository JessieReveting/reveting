# Breach of Protocol Episode Template

## Working Title

Use a title built around a durable strategic theme, not a temporary headline.

## Strategic Theme

State the argument Ridgeback wants the audience to leave with.

## Public Discussion Topics

1. What changed in the threat landscape?
2. What defensive blind spot does it reveal?
3. What should security leaders do differently now?

## Breaking Threat Brief

Reserve a short section for the most important cyberattack, vulnerability, AI development, or policy story of the week.

## CTA Direction

Default to a Ridgeback CTA that fits the level of audience intent.
