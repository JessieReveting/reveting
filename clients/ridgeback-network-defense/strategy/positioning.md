# Ridgeback Positioning

## Overview

Ridgeback Network Defense focuses on cyber strategy, preemptive defense, automated attack interdiction, and active attack disruption.

Its central position is that cybersecurity should impose cost, uncertainty, and disadvantage on attackers before they can act successfully.

Ridgeback helps organizations regain the initiative instead of waiting for alerts, incidents, or successful compromise.

## Core Strategic Narrative

Traditional cybersecurity programs are often designed around detection, containment, and response.

Ridgeback's point of view is that defenders should act earlier.

AI is accelerating the tempo of cyber conflict, compressing attacker timelines and exposing the limits of defensive architectures built for a slower era.

Ridgeback content should help executives, boards, CISOs, operators, and security teams understand how to move from reactive defense toward preemptive action.

## Commercial Bridge

Ridgeback content should help the audience understand the cost of waiting, the value of earlier action, and the operational logic behind preemptive defense.

The commercial bridge should feel like:

- strategic education first
- clear implications second
- a business conversation only when the audience is ready for it
