# Ridgeback Audiences

| Audience | What They Care About | What Content Should Help Them Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CEOs and executive leadership | Business risk, initiative, resilience, and speed of response | Understand why reactive security leaves the business exposed |
| Corporate boards | Oversight, accountability, and strategic cyber posture | Ask better questions about timing, initiative, and readiness |
| CISOs and security leaders | Defensive architecture, staffing pressure, and cyber strategy | Reframe programs around preemptive action instead of alert volume |
| Security architects and operators | Control design, response speed, and implementation reality | Translate strategy into operational guardrails and earlier intervention |
| SOC leaders and analysts | Workload, prioritization, and signal quality | See how earlier disruption reduces downstream burden |
| Critical infrastructure organizations | Resilience, uptime, and national-risk implications | Understand why trusted access and attacker speed change consequence models |
| Government and regulated enterprises | Compliance, risk exposure, and mission continuity | Connect policy and control obligations to preemptive defensive action |
| Cybersecurity strategists and practitioners | Threat evolution, doctrine, and practical response models | Build a more durable point of view about how defenders regain initiative |
