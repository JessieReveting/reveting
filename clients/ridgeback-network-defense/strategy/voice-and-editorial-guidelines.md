# Ridgeback Voice And Editorial Guidelines

## Voice

- Strategic
- Direct
- Intelligent
- Credible
- Clear enough for executives
- Substantive enough for practitioners
- Focused on strategy rather than product promotion
- Willing to challenge conventional cybersecurity assumptions

## Avoid

- Generic fear-based cybersecurity content
- Overly technical jargon without strategic context
- Empty AI commentary
- Vendor-heavy promotional language
- Claims that one technology solves every security problem

## Editorial Decision Rule

If a draft sounds like a security vendor pitch before it sounds like a sharp strategic argument, rewrite it until the analysis leads and the promotion follows.
