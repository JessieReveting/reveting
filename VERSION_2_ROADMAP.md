# Version 2 Roadmap

Version 1 is read-only. Do not begin Version 2 implementation until explicitly approved.

## AI Operations Manager

- Cross-show reasoning over normalized snapshots.
- Plain-English daily production questions and answers.
- Explainable recommendations with source evidence links.

## Production Manager

- Production checklist tracking per show and episode.
- SOP-aware readiness states.
- Ownership and escalation routing.

## Approval Workflows

- Human review queues for every proposed change.
- Explicit approve/reject flows before any external write.
- Change previews showing before/after state.

## Controlled Automation

Possible future actions after approval:

- Update Google Calendar events.
- Update HighLevel records.
- Send Gmail messages.
- Create Drive folders.
- Send Slack or internal notifications.

## Historical Reporting

- Audit snapshots over time.
- Trend lines for issue counts and health scores.
- Per-client and per-show reliability views.

## Operational Analytics

- Booking-to-calendar sync reliability.
- Guest readiness and asset completeness.
- Recurring issue detection by show/client.

## Predictive Issue Detection

- Flag likely missing assets before they become urgent.
- Predict no-show or incomplete-production risk from historical patterns.

## Daily Production Briefings

- Morning brief of upcoming episodes, blockers, and owner actions.
- Critical issue escalation digest.
- Show-by-show readiness summary.

## Cross-Client Dashboard

- Unified production health across Reveting clients.
- Drilldowns by client, show, episode, asset, and SOP stage.
