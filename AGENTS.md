# Project Memory

Until Jessie says otherwise, quality check Reveting operations work against:

- `docs/sops/REVETING_SOP_MEMORY.md`
- `docs/sops/checklists/reveting-operations-qc.md`

The raw extracted SOP text is stored locally in `docs/sops/source-text/` and is intentionally ignored from Git because the SOP examples include contact details.

Standing Reveting rules:

- `ww@reveting.com` is the calendar source of truth.
- Production Assistant owns calendar accuracy, descriptions, show naming, and preserving the original guest submission.
- Confirm show timezone, channel stack, client outline link, show duration, and pre-show time from the Total Deliverables Document before touching an event.
- Never guess the channel stack.
- Never email a guest raw submitted topics, and never put raw submitted topics directly into a calendar description.
- Topics must be edited to fit the show strategy before guest-facing use.
- LinkedIn follower fit gate: 5,000+ preferred; 1,000-4,999 requires Production + Marketing Lead confirmation; below 1,000 is no-go unless there is no better option.
- All events are set in Eastern Time unless the show-specific source of truth says otherwise.
- Email 5 must go out within 1 hour of show end with AI clips, full recording, transcript, and organized Drive link when available.
- Final post-production email should go out 24-36 hours after the show and must not exceed 36 hours.
- When in doubt, WhatsApp Jessie.
